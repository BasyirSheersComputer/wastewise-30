# Malaysian F&B Industry Pain Points: Top 10% Revenue Earners Research

## Executive Summary

This document compiles academic research, journal articles, and industry studies that detail the specific pain points and challenges faced by the **top 10% revenue earners** in the Malaysian F&B industry. These high-revenue establishments (RM 2M-10M+ annually) face unique operational, technological, and financial challenges that directly impact waste management, inventory optimization, and overall business efficiency. The research reveals that despite their higher revenue, these establishments still experience significant inefficiencies and waste, making them prime candidates for advanced technology solutions.
