# WasteWise AI - Onboarding Guide

## Overview

This guide outlines the comprehensive onboarding process designed to get new users up and running with WasteWise AI quickly and efficiently. Our onboarding process is optimized for maximum user engagement and rapid time-to-value.

## Onboarding Flow

### 1. Pre-Onboarding (Landing Page)

**Objective**: Capture interest and qualify prospects

**Components**:
- **Hero Section**: Clear value proposition with pain point amplification
- **Demo Button**: Interactive product demonstration
- **Social Proof**: Customer testimonials and case studies
- **Trial CTA**: "Start Free Trial" with no credit card required

**Key Features**:
- RM 3.2B waste problem highlighting
- 35-45% waste reduction guarantee
- 30-day free trial promotion
- Interactive demo access

### 2. Signup Process

**Objective**: Convert visitors to trial users

**Components**:
- **Simple Form**: Minimal required fields
- **Email Verification**: Secure account creation
- **Welcome Email**: Immediate engagement
- **Trial Activation**: Instant access to platform

**User Experience**:
- One-page signup form
- Real-time validation
- Clear progress indicators
- Mobile-responsive design

### 3. Onboarding Form (3-Step Process)

**Objective**: Collect business information for personalized experience

#### Step 1: Business Information
- **Business Type**: Restaurant, Café, Fast Food, etc.
- **Number of Locations**: Single or multi-location
- **Annual Revenue**: Revenue range selection
- **Visual Progress**: Clear step indicators

#### Step 2: Goals & Data Sources
- **Primary Goals**: Multi-select options
  - Reduce food waste
  - Cut operational costs
  - Improve efficiency
  - Better analytics & reporting
  - Regulatory compliance
  - Sustainability goals
- **Current Data Sources**: Existing systems integration
  - POS System
  - Inventory Management
  - Accounting Software
  - Manual Tracking
  - Supplier Data
  - No existing system

#### Step 3: Team & Setup
- **Team Size**: Employee count ranges
- **Timezone**: Location-based settings
- **Setup Summary**: Review collected information
- **Completion**: Ready to access dashboard

### 4. Welcome Experience

**Objective**: Immediate value demonstration

**Components**:
- **Welcome Screen**: Personalized greeting
- **Feature Preview**: Key capabilities overview
- **Quick Start Guide**: First steps tutorial
- **Demo Access**: Interactive product tour

**Key Elements**:
- 30-day trial countdown
- Feature highlights
- Success metrics preview
- Next steps guidance
