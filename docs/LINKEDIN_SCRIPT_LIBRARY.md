## Discovery Call Scripts

### Opening (5 minutes)

**Rapport Building:**
```
"Thanks for taking the time! How's [Company] doing these days? I saw you recently opened [location/achievement] - congratulations!"

[Pause for response, show genuine interest]

"Today I'd like to understand your waste management challenges, then share what we're building, and see if you'd be interested in being one of our first pilot partners. Does that work?"
```

### Discovery Questions (15-20 minutes)

#### Pain Point Discovery
1. "What's your biggest challenge with waste management right now?"
2. "How much are you spending on waste monthly?"
3. "How do you currently forecast inventory needs?"
4. "What impact does waste have on your profitability?"

#### Current Process
5. "What systems do you use for inventory management?"
6. "How often do you place orders?"
7. "What's your biggest frustration with the current process?"

#### Authority & Budget
8. "Who else is involved in decisions like this?"
9. "What would need to happen for you to invest in a solution?"
10. "What's your timeline for addressing this?"

#### Decision Criteria
11. "What would an ideal solution look like?"
12. "What's prevented you from solving this before?"
13. "What would make this a no-brainer decision?"

### Value Introduction (10 minutes)

**Transition (Beta Positioning):**
```
"Based on what you've shared, I think WasteWise could help because [specific match to their pain point].

Here's what we're building: [explain solution]

Given [Company]'s situation - [X] locations, [Y] monthly waste cost - if our AI forecasting works as designed, you could potentially see RM [calculated savings] monthly savings.

That's exactly why we're looking for pilot partners - to prove this works with real Malaysian F&B chains like yours."
```

### Closing (5 minutes)

**If Qualified (Beta Close):**
```
"This sounds like a great fit for our pilot program! 

Here's what I'm proposing: We start with a 60-day free trial - full platform access, dedicated setup support, and your feedback shapes the product.

In exchange, you get lifetime early adopter pricing (50% off) if you decide to continue.

Since we're only taking 3-5 pilot partners, I'd love to get you started if you're interested. What do you think?"
```

**If Not Qualified:**
```
"I appreciate your time. Based on our conversation, [reason it's not a fit right now]. 

Would it make sense to reconnect in [timeline] when [condition changes]?"
```

## Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "We're not interested"

**Response:**
```
"No problem - I appreciate your honesty. If waste costs become a priority, I'm here.

Mind if I check back in 3 months? Or would you prefer I remove you from my list?"
```

### Objection 2: "Too expensive"

**Response:**
```
"I understand cost is a concern. Let me put this in perspective:

Most chains see RM 100K-500K monthly savings. At RM 5,000/month, that's a 20-100x ROI.

For example, [Similar Chain] saves RM 150K monthly - that's 30x their investment.

What would need to happen for this to be a no-brainer?"
```

### Objection 3: "We're not ready"

**Response:**
```
"Understandable. What would need to change for you to be ready?

We can start with a 30-day trial - no commitment. If it doesn't work, you walk away.

When would be a better time to discuss?"
```

### Objection 4: "Send me more info"

**Response:**
```
"Happy to! I'll send over:
- Our case study on [Similar Chain]
- ROI calculator
- Platform demo video

Would you be open to a quick 15-min call after you review? I can answer any questions."
```

### Objection 5: "We already have a system"

**Response:**
```
"That's great you have something in place. What's working well with your current system?

[Wait for response]

Many chains supplement their existing systems with WasteWise for AI forecasting specifically. We integrate with most systems.

Would you be open to seeing how it could enhance what you already have?"
```

### Objection 6: "I need to think about it"

**Response:**
```
"Of course - it's a big decision. What specifically would you like to think about?

I'm happy to answer any questions or provide additional information to help you decide.

Is there anything holding you back I can address?"
```
