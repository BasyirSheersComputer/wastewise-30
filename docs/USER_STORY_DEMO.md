# 🍽️ WasteWise Demo - User Story

## 👤 **Meet Sarah, Restaurant Manager**

Sarah manages "Spice Garden," a popular Malaysian restaurant chain with 5 locations. She's struggling with:
- **30% food waste** costing RM 15,000 monthly
- **Inconsistent inventory tracking** leading to stockouts
- **No data-driven insights** for menu optimization
- **Manual waste tracking** that's time-consuming and error-prone

## 🎯 **Sarah's Goals**
- Reduce food waste by 50%
- Increase profit margins by 15%
- Streamline inventory management
- Make data-driven menu decisions

---

## 🚀 **Day 1: First Discovery**

### **Morning - Dashboard Overview**
Sarah opens http://localhost:5173 and sees the WasteWise dashboard:

**📊 Key Metrics at a Glance:**
- **Monthly Waste Cost**: RM 12,450 (down from RM 15,000!)
- **Waste Reduction**: 17% improvement
- **Top Waste Items**: Curry Chicken, Beef Rendang, Laksa
- **AI Recommendations**: 3 new suggestions available

**🎯 Sarah's Reaction:** *"Wow! I can see exactly where our money is going. The AI recommendations look promising."*

### **Afternoon - Exploring AI Insights**
Sarah clicks on "AI Recommendations" and discovers:

**🤖 Strategic Insights:**
1. **Menu Optimization**: "Consider reducing Curry Chicken portion size by 10% - could save RM 2,400/month"
2. **Inventory Alert**: "Beef Rendang ingredients expiring in 3 days - prioritize menu placement"
3. **Staff Training**: "Waste events peak during dinner rush - schedule waste reduction training"

**💡 Sarah's Insight:** *"The AI is telling me exactly what I need to focus on. This is like having a consultant!"*

---

## 📈 **Day 2: Deep Dive into Analytics**

### **Morning - Waste Tracking**
Sarah explores the waste tracking module:

**🗑️ Waste Analysis:**
- **Curry Chicken**: 15kg wasted this week (RM 450 loss)
- **Beef Rendang**: 8kg wasted (RM 320 loss)
- **Laksa**: 12kg wasted (RM 360 loss)

**📊 Patterns Discovered:**
- Most waste occurs on weekends
- Over-preparation is the main cause
- Staff training needed for portion control

**🎯 Sarah's Action:** *"I'll implement the AI's suggestion to reduce Curry Chicken portions and schedule staff training."*

### **Afternoon - Menu Optimization**
Sarah reviews the menu analytics:

**🍽️ Menu Performance:**
- **Top Performers**: Nasi Lemak, Chicken Satay, Teh Tarik
- **Underperformers**: Beef Rendang (high waste), Laksa (low margin)
- **Recommendations**: 
  - Increase Nasi Lemak price by RM 2
  - Reduce Beef Rendang portion size
  - Promote Chicken Satay during lunch

**💡 Sarah's Decision:** *"I'll adjust the menu based on these insights and track the results."*
